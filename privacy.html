Privacy

Your answers to the 40 statements are scored in your browser. They are not sent to Dr Elijah Ting or stored on this site unless you later choose to paste them into a message yourself.

If you use the enquiry form, your name, email and message are sent so that the practice can reply. Those enquiry emails are copied to a second inbox used for practice administration, under Malaysia’s Personal Data Protection Act. They are not used for any other purpose.
